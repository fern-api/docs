## Startup banner on root `--help`

<ChangelogTags>features, generators.yml</ChangelogTags>

The `banner` option prints ASCII art on root `--help`. It can be a solid color, a multi-stop RGB gradient (`horizontal`, `vertical`, or `diagonal`), one color per line, or pre-colored ANSI art. Set `banner.animate: true` to play a short shimmer in interactive terminals.
