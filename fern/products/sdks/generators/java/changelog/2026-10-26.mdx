## 4.35.0
**`(feat):`** Expose `maxConcurrentRequests(int)` on the generated root client builders (`<Client>Builder`,
`Async<Client>Builder`, and the multi-auth builder stages) so the in-flight request cap added in
4.34.0 can be set without constructing `ClientOptions` by hand, matching `timeout` and `maxRetries`.
Subclasses can override the new `setConcurrencyLimit(ClientOptions.Builder)` hook.


## 4.34.0
**`(feat):`** Add `ClientOptions.Builder.maxConcurrentRequests(int)`, an opt-in cap on how many requests the SDK has in
flight at once. It applies to both the sync and the async client (OkHttp's dispatcher limits only bound async
calls, and its connection pool only bounds idle connections) and also to an OkHttpClient supplied via
`httpClient(...)`. A request holds a slot until its response body is closed; callers waiting for a slot are
bounded by the call timeout, and retries do not hold a slot during their backoff delay. Implemented by the new
generated `core/ConcurrencyLimitingInterceptor`.
