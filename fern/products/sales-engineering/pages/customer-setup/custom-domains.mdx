---
title: Custom Domains
description: How to add a custom domain for a customer's Fern documentation site.
---

Follow these steps to configure a custom domain for a customer.

## Prerequisites

You will need access to the [Vercel project for prod.ferndocs.com](https://vercel.com/buildwithfern/prod.ferndocs.com/settings/domains).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update docs.yml">
    Have the customer update their `docs.yml` with the custom domain they want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the custom subpath">
    Ask the customer to share the custom subpath they want to publish to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the domain in Vercel">
    Add the domain to [Vercel domain settings](https://vercel.com/buildwithfern/prod.ferndocs.com/settings/domains).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share DNS records">
    Send the customer the required DNS updates (TXT records, CNAME) so they can point their domain to Fern.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for SSL">
    Wait until the SSL certificate generates successfully in Vercel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Republish docs">
    Have the customer republish their docs to apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
