title: PostHog Analytics
description: How to look at raw customer feedback data in PostHog.
---

Use PostHog to view raw feedback submitted by users on customer documentation sites.

## Viewing feedback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open PostHog">
    Go to [us.posthog.com](https://us.posthog.com/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Activity">
    Open the **Activity** section from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by event">
    Select the event: `feedback_submitted`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by customer">
    Add a filter for **Person properties** → `Host` = `{customer-name}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the data">
    Adjust the time range, look at other feature flags, and explore the filters to get the data you need.
  </Step>
</Steps>
