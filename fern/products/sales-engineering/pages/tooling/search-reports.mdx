## Generating a search report from Algolia

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Algolia">
    Go to the [Algolia Dashboard](https://www.algolia.com/dashboard) and navigate to the **Fern Docs Search** application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to analytics">
    Go to **Analytics** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by customer site">
    Use the filters to narrow results to the specific customer site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View searches">
    Navigate to the **Searches** tab to see what users are searching for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export CSV">
    Export the search data as a CSV for further analysis or sharing with the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
