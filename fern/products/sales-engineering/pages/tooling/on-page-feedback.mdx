<Warning>
This process is **deprecated** and is no longer needed for new customers. It is documented here for historical reference only.
</Warning>

## Steps (legacy)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Fern Zapier organization">
    Go to the Fern organization for Zapier: [https://zapier.com/app/assets/zaps](https://zapier.com/app/assets/zaps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy a template Zap">
    Copy the template of another company's Zap and modify it:
    1. Change the **Filter conditions** (step 2) to the URL of the new customer.
    2. Change the input argument **Slack channel** to a new Slack channel's channel ID that you've created for on-page feedback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the feedback flow">
    Test that the on-page feedback goes through. It may take a few minutes. You can speed it up by adding the Slack app to the channel before testing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to the testing Zap">
    If successful, add the customer URL to the **AAA Testing Notifs Zap**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
For Google Sheets feedback, copy a customer like Webflow or LaunchDarkly as a template.
</Note>
