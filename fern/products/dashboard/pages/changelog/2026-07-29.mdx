## Dashboard agent chat history

<ChangelogTags>ai, navigation</ChangelogTags>

You can now revisit past conversations with the Dashboard agent. The left sidebar has a **Browse** and **Chat** toggle: **Chat** lists your conversations grouped by recency, with a search field and a **New chat** button. Each conversation gets an automatically generated title and its own URL, so a chat can be reopened, linked, or shared with teammates.

Select **Chat** in the sidebar of the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/).
