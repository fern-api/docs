## Fern token management

You can now create, rename, and delete Fern tokens directly from the Dashboard. Name your tokens to keep track of where each one is used, and rotate old tokens when needed.

To get started, go to **API Tokens** in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/) sidebar.
