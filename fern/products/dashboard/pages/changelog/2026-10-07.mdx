## Feedback tab views for pages and Ask Fern

<ChangelogTags>ai</ChangelogTags>

The **Feedback** tab in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/) now has a separate view for each kind of feedback: **Pages**, **Ask Fern**, and **Code issues**. Summary tiles report how often pages and Ask Fern answers were found helpful as separate rates.

- Ask Fern ratings now include the question the reader asked and the answer they rated, with a link to the full conversation. Ratings from the Ask Fern side panel are counted as Ask Fern feedback, including past ratings.
- Turn on **Include votes without details** to also count thumbs-up and thumbs-down votes that came without a reason or comment.
