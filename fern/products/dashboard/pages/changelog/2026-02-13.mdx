## PDF Export

You can now export your entire documentation site as a PDF directly from the Fern Dashboard.

Each export includes:
- A cover page with a customizable title and subtitle
- An auto-generated table of contents
- All content pages
- Optional headers and footers with page number placeholders

To get started, go to **Settings** > **PDF Exporter** in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/). Exports run asynchronously and may take a few minutes for larger sites.
