[Admins](/learn/dashboard/configuration/permissions) can invite new members to a Fern organization from the [Fern Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/). Each member is assigned a role that controls their Dashboard and CLI access.

Your [plan](https://buildwithfern.com/pricing) determines the number of members you can add:

| Plan       | Team members |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| Hobby      | 2            |
| Pro        | 5            |
| Enterprise | Custom       |

## Invite a member

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Dashboard">

Navigate to the [Fern Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/) and select **Members** from the sidebar.
</Step>

<Step title="Send an invite">

Select **Invite member**, enter the new member's email address, and choose a [role](/learn/dashboard/configuration/permissions): Admin, Editor, or Viewer.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure CLI access (Editors only)">

If you selected the Editor role, you can toggle CLI access. Editors with CLI access can publish SDKs and documentation to production. All Editors can deploy previews regardless of this setting.
</Step>
</Steps>

The invited member receives an email with a link to join your organization.

## Change a member's role

To update a member's role, open the **Members** page in the Dashboard and select a new role from the dropdown next to their name. Role changes take effect immediately.

## Remove a member

To remove a member, open the **Members** page and select **Remove** next to their name. Removed members lose access to the Dashboard and CLI for your organization immediately.
