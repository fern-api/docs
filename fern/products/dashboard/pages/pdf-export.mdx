title: PDF export
description: Export your documentation site as a PDF from the Fern Dashboard.
---

Export your complete documentation site as a PDF directly from the [Fern Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/). The generated PDF includes a cover page, table of contents with clickable links, and all content pages with headers and footers.

## Export a PDF

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Dashboard">

Navigate to the [Fern Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/) and select your documentation site.
</Step>

<Step title="Launch the PDF Exporter">

In the **PDF Exporter** card, select **Launch** to open the export dialog.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure export settings">

Optionally override the default settings to customize your PDF:

- **Cover title** and **subtitle**: Customize or hide the text on the cover page.
- **Hide cover footer**: Toggle the "Generated by Fern" footer on the cover page.
- **Headers and footers**: Add text to the top or bottom of each content page using templates with `{pageIndex}` and `{totalPages}` placeholders.

If you don't override a setting, the site default is used.
</Step>

<Step title="Export">

Select **Export as PDF** to start the export. Exports run asynchronously and may take a few minutes for large documentation sites. You can track the status in the **Exports** section of the dialog.
</Step>

<Step title="Download">

When the export completes, select **Open** to download the PDF.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Header and footer templates

Header and footer templates support two placeholders:

| Placeholder | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `{pageIndex}` | The current page number |
| `{totalPages}` | The total number of content pages |

For example, a footer template of `Page {pageIndex} of {totalPages}` renders as "Page 3 of 12" on the third content page of a 12-page document.
