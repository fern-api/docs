## Find answers

Users can chat with [Ask Fern](/learn/ask-fern/getting-started/what-is-ask-fern) to get instant answers from your documentation. It's available as an embedded chat widget, via the [API](/learn/ask-fern/api-reference/overview) for custom integrations, and as a [Slack app](/learn/ask-fern/features/slack-app) for your community workspace. Ask Fern understands context and provides citations so users can verify information and explore further.

## Create and update content

[Fern Scribe](/learn/docs/ai/scribe) is a technical writing agent available in beta via Slack. Tag `@Scribe` to request documentation updates, and it drafts targeted edits via GitHub pull requests. Scribe learns your writing style and adapts to your team's workflow.

## Optimize for AI

Your site is automatically optimized for AI tools and search engines. Fern serves Markdown instead of HTML to AI agents, reducing token consumption and helping these tools process your content faster. Your site hosts [`llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`](/learn/docs/ai/llms-txt) files so LLMs can index your documentation efficiently.

Developers using your products can connect AI clients like Claude Desktop, Cursor, and Windsurf directly to your docs through the [MCP server](/learn/docs/ai/mcp-server) that Fern hosts for your site. This gives users up-to-date information about your product right where they need it.
