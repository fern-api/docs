## Enable Ask Fern

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure `docs.yml`">
  Add the `ai-search` configuration to your `docs.yml` file to declare where Ask Fern should be available:

  ```yaml docs.yml
  ai-search:
    location:
      - docs
      - slack # Or discord
  ```

  - `docs` — enables the Ask Fern search panel on your documentation site
  - `slack` — enables Ask Fern in Slack ([setup](/learn/docs/ai-features/ask-fern/slack-app))
  - `discord` — enables Ask Fern in Discord ([setup](/learn/docs/ai-features/ask-fern/discord-bot))

  Most teams enable `docs` along with either `slack` or `discord`.
    </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Ask AI in the Dashboard">
    Open the [Fern Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/). Navigate to **Settings**, and click **Enable** on the Ask AI card. This triggers an automatic reindex of your documentation content.

    <Note>
      After you enable Ask Fern, it needs to index your content before the [side panel](/learn/docs/ai-features/ask-fern/overview) can appear on your site. This typically takes a few minutes, though sites with extensive custom components may take longer. 
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up Slack or Discord">
  Connect Ask Fern to [Slack](/learn/docs/ai-features/ask-fern/slack-app) or [Discord](/learn/docs/ai-features/ask-fern/discord-bot) so your users can ask questions directly from chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your configuration (optional)">
  Finetune Ask Fern's behavior by adjusting settings like additional content sources, system prompts, and model selection. See [Configuration](#configuration) for the full list of properties.
  </Step>
</Steps>
