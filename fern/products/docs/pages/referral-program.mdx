This referral program is for active Fern customers who naturally come across teams facing similar API documentation or SDK challenges. If someone in your network would benefit from Fern, we're happy to take an introduction and handle everything from there.

## How it works

### Intro reward

Introduce us to a decision maker at a company that takes a discovery call with our team, and we'll send you **$100**.

### Customer reward

If the company you referred signs with Fern, you get:

- **$750** for a [Team (Docs) or Pro (SDKs) plan](https://buildwithfern.com/pricing) signup
- **$1,000** for an [Enterprise plan](https://buildwithfern.com/pricing) signup

### Volume bonus

Refer 5 qualified introductions and we'll enter you in a raffle to win **$5,000**.

## How to refer

Share the booking link from your referral email with anyone you think would benefit from Fern. When they schedule a discovery call, they'll be able to indicate that they were referred by you. 

## Terms and restrictions

To qualify, a referral must meet the following:

- **New prospects only** — the referred company isn't an existing Fern customer or already in the Fern sales pipeline.
- **Legitimate businesses only** — the referred company must be a legitimate business entity (e.g., LLC or equivalent), have more than one team member, and generate revenue.
- **One referral per person** — you may only refer the same individual once, and they must not already exist in Fern's system.
- **Rewards are non-transferable** — gift cards and rewards can't be transferred to another party.
- **Active customers only** — this program is exclusively for current Fern customers.
- **Monthly payouts** — referral rewards are processed and paid out once per month.

Fern reserves the right to modify or discontinue this program at any time. All qualification decisions are at the discretion of Fern's sales team.
