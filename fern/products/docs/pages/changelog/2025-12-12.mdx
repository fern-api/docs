## Password protection

You can now protect your documentation site with a shared password. When enabled, visitors must enter the password before they can access any content.

![Password protection prompt](password-protection.png)

Password protection is ideal for early stage documentation, internal docs that don't require individual user tracking, or temporary access control during development.

To enable password protection on your docs site, contact [Fern Support](mailto:support@buildwithfern.com).

[Learn more](/learn/docs/authentication/overview#password-protection) about password protection and other authentication options.
