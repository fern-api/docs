## Paginated and expandable tables

<ChangelogTags>components</ChangelogTags>

You can now paginate long tables and collapse them to a preview. The `<PaginatedTable>` and `<PaginatedSearchableTable>` components split rows across pages with previous and next controls, the `pageSize` prop sets rows per page, and the `expandable` prop adds a **Show all** control that expands the table to every row. Pagination composes with the existing sticky and searchable table features.
