## Real page titles for external sitemap results

<ChangelogTags>search</ChangelogTags>

Search results for pages indexed from `experimental.external-sitemaps` now show each page's real title instead of one derived from its URL, so help center and community pages are findable by topic.

- Fern's crawler identifies itself as `FernBot/1.0 (+https://buildwithfern.com)` and follows `robots.txt` and `Crawl-delay`, so site owners can allow or restrict it.
- URLs that carry their language in a query parameter such as `lang`, `hl`, or `_LANG` are routed to the matching site locale.
- A temporarily unavailable sitemap no longer removes its pages from search.
