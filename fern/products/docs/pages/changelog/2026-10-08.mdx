## Dynamic OG images on by default

<ChangelogTags>seo</ChangelogTags>

Sites that set no site-wide `og:image` or `twitter:image` now get a generated preview image for every page, so links shared in Slack, X, and LinkedIn show a card image instead of a blank placeholder. Pages with their own `og:image` in frontmatter keep it. Set `og:dynamic: false` under `metadata` in `docs.yml` to opt out.
