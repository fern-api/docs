## Custom roles claim for SSO

<ChangelogTags>security</ChangelogTags>

You can now source RBAC roles from a custom claim on the WorkOS access token when authenticating with [SSO](/learn/docs/authentication/setup/sso). Ask Fern to set `roles_claim` to read roles from a claim such as a directory-group claim. When omitted, roles come from the WorkOS organization role.
