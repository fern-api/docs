## Card images in Fern Editor

<ChangelogTags>developer-tools, components</ChangelogTags>

You can now configure a Card's image directly in Fern Editor. The Card properties panel includes a **Source** field and an **Image Position** selector (`top`, `left`, `right`, or `bottom`). Repo-relative image paths resolve in the editor, and the preview matches the published Card component.
