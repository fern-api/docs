## Mobile table of contents bar

Pages using the [`guide`](/learn/docs/configuration/page-level-settings#layout) and [`overview`](/learn/docs/configuration/page-level-settings#layout) layouts can now display a sticky table of contents bar below the header on mobile and tablet viewports. The bar shows a scroll progress indicator and the current heading, and expands to the full table of contents when tapped.

Enable it by adding `mobile-toc: true` under `layout` in `docs.yml`:

```yaml docs.yml
layout:
  mobile-toc: true
```
