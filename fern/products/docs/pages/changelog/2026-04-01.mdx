## Shakespearean English mode

Modern docs sites can feel too casual, lacking the gravitas that your product deserves. Add `?shakespeare=true` to any Fern Docs page URL to transform it into Shakespearean English, bringing formality and weight to your documentation. The mode applies a parchment visual theme and translates page text client-side.
