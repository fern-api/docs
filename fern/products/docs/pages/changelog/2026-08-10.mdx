## Match `<If>` products and versions by display name

<ChangelogTags>components, navigation, bug-fix</ChangelogTags>

`<If products={[...]}>` and `<If versions={[...]}>` now match a product or version by its display name, in addition to its slug and its id. A gate written against the name readers see in the version or product switcher, such as `<If versions={["v1 (Legacy)"]}>`, previously matched nothing and dropped its content from the published site. The default version, which is served at your docs basepath and has no slug of its own, can now be targeted by its display name.
