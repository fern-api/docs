## Match `<If>` products and versions by display name

<ChangelogTags>components, navigation</ChangelogTags>

`<If products={[...]}>` and `<If versions={[...]}>` now match a product or version by its display name, as well as by its slug or its id. You can gate content using the name readers see in the product or version switcher, such as `<If versions={["v1 (Legacy)"]}>`. This includes the default version, which is served at your docs basepath and has no slug of its own.
