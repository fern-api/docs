## Remote paths

<ChangelogTags>docs.yml</ChangelogTags>

You can now serve a path prefix on your Fern docs domain from another HTTPS service you run. List each prefix and its destination under `remote-paths` in `docs.yml`, and Fern reverse-proxies those requests while serving the rest of the site.

```yml docs.yml
remote-paths:
  - path: /ui
    destination: https://portal.example.com/ui
```
