## Preview docs as a role

<ChangelogTags>security</ChangelogTags>

You can now preview a role-gated docs site as a specific viewer. On a preview link, a role selector lets you pick one or more roles (or anonymous) and re-renders the site with that viewer's [role-based visibility](/learn/docs/authentication/features/rbac#preview-as-a-role) applied, including nav pruning, `<If />` blocks, and role-gated tabs and products. With no selection, the preview continues to show all content.
