## Locale-aware Ask Fern datasources

<ChangelogTags>ai, search</ChangelogTags>

Websites listed under `ai-search.datasources` in `docs.yml` are now indexed automatically every time you publish, using the pages listed in each website's sitemap, and removed from Ask Fern's index when you delete them from the list. On localized sites, each crawled page is assigned a locale from its `hreflang`, its URL path prefix, or the site default, and Ask Fern answers readers from pages in their current locale. Set `locale` on a datasource to assign every page of that website to one locale.

```yaml docs.yml
ai-search:
  datasources:
    - url: https://help.example.com/ja-jp
      locale: ja
```
