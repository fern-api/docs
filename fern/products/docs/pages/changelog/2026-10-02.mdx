## Draggable Ask AI chat widget

<ChangelogTags>ai, components</ChangelogTags>

Visitors can now move the standalone `AskAiChat` widget anywhere on the page. Drag the card by its header, or drag the launcher button itself and the card follows. The widget stays inside the viewport, and the card opens above or below the launcher depending on where there's room. Dragging is on by default in `@fern-api/search-widget` 0.6.0; pass `draggableCard={false}` to keep the chat pinned to its `placement` corner.
