## Standalone Ask AI widget

<ChangelogTags>ai, search</ChangelogTags>

You can now embed the Ask Fern chat experience in any React application with the `AskAiChat` component from the [`@fern-api/search-widget`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fern-api/search-widget) package. It renders a floating card anchored to a corner of the viewport, becomes a bottom drawer on small screens, and matches your product with the `theme` and `accentColor` props.
