## Ask Fern query log retention

<ChangelogTags>ai, security</ChangelogTags>

You can now purge Ask Fern end-user query logs on a schedule instead of retaining them indefinitely. Enable **Automatically purge old query logs** on the **Query log retention** card and set a retention period between 1 and 3650 days. Purged queries no longer appear in Ask Fern analytics.

To configure this, go to your site's **Settings** page in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com/).
