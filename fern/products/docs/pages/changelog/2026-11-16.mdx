## Tab menus

<ChangelogTags>navigation, docs.yml</ChangelogTags>

You can now turn a header tab into a menu of other tabs. Add a `tab-menu` to the tab in `docs.yml`, then give each menu tab its own `- tab:` entry in `navigation` with a `layout` or `variants`.

- Menu entries can be tabs, `group` entries with a header, or external links.
- Long menus split into columns with `tab-menu-column-height`, or site-wide with `layout.tab-menu.default-column-height`.
- Menus open on hover.
- Tab `variants` lists also accept `group` entries and links.

```yaml docs.yml
tabs:
  products:
    display-name: Products
    tab-menu:
      - tab: agents
        display-name: Agents
      - tab: studio
        display-name: Studio
```
