When you're ready to move on from your Fern Docs project, here are some resources to help you wrap up.

## Export your content

You can export your documentation content at any time. Your Markdown files and configuration are stored in your GitHub repository, so you always have full access to your source files.

## Cancel your subscription

To cancel your Fern subscription, visit the [Fern Dashboard](https://dashboard.buildwithfern.com) and navigate to your organization settings.

## Contact support

If you have questions or need assistance, [reach out to our team](https://buildwithfern.com/contact).
