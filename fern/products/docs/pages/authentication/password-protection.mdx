title: Password protection
subtitle: Simple access control for your documentation
---

Password protection provides a straightforward way to restrict access to your documentation site. Visitors must enter a shared password before they can view any content.

<Note>Password protection doesn't support Role-Based Access Control, API Key Injection, or per-user authentication.</Note>

## When to use password protection

Password protection works well for:

- **Early stage documentation**: Restrict access while your docs are still in development
- **Internal previews**: Share documentation with stakeholders before public release
- **Simple access control**: When you need basic protection without user management

For more advanced access control with per-user authentication, consider [RBAC](/learn/docs/authentication/rbac) or [SSO](/learn/docs/authentication/sso).

## How it works

When password protection is enabled, visitors see a password prompt before accessing your documentation. After entering the correct password, they can browse all pages on your site during their session.

## Setting up password protection

To enable password protection on your docs site, contact support@buildwithfern.com or reach out via your dedicated Slack channel. Fern will configure the password for your site.
