---
title: Intercom
description: Learn how to integrate Intercom with Fern Docs!
---

To add Intercom to your Docs, you need your Intercom `app_id`, also known as the Intercom workspace ID. This is a unique code assigned to your app when you create it in Intercom. You may also configure a custom Intercom endpoint.

<Steps>
	<Step title="Get your Intercom app ID">

		Your app ID is available under [Settings > Workspace > General](https://app.intercom.com/a/apps/_/settings/workspace/general) in the "Workspace name & time zone" tab.

		See [Intercom's FAQ](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8771110-getting-started-faqs#h_c12f89cf9d) for visual instructions.

	</Step>

	<Step title="Add Intercom to docs.yml">

		In your `docs.yml` file, add your Intercom config:

		<CodeBlock title="docs.yml">
		```yaml
		analytics:
		  intercom:
		    app-id: ${INTERCOM_APP_ID} # reads your app id from environment variables
		    # Optional
		    endpoint: ${INTERCOM_ENDPOINT} # e.g. https://intercom.custom-instance.com
		```
		</CodeBlock>

	</Step>
</Steps>
