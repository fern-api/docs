Optimize your documentation's search visibility and social media presence by configuring SEO metadata at the page or site level. Properly configured metadata helps search engines understand your content and creates compelling previews when your pages are shared on social platforms.

## Best practices

When configuring SEO metadata, follow these guidelines for optimal results:

**Titles**: Keep titles between 50-60 characters. Make them unique, descriptive, and relevant to the page content. Include your brand name for consistency.

**Descriptions**: Aim for 150-160 characters. Write compelling copy that encourages clicks while accurately describing the content. Avoid keyword stuffing.

**Images**: Use high-quality images sized at 1200x630 pixels for optimal display across social platforms. Ensure images are relevant to the content and include your branding.

**Consistency**: Use consistent branding across all metadata fields. Your `og:site_name`, `twitter:site`, and `twitter:handle` should align with your brand identity.

**Testing**: Use tools like [Facebook's Sharing Debugger](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/) and [Twitter's Card Validator](https://cards-dev.twitter.com/validator) to preview how your pages will appear when shared.

## Page metadata

Set SEO properties in each page's [frontmatter](/docs/configuration/page-level-settings) to control how individual pages appear in search results and social media shares. Page-level metadata takes precedence over site-wide settings.
