Modern documentation sites can feel too casual, lacking the gravitas that your product deserves. Shakespeare mode addresses this by rendering your documentation in Shakespearean English with a parchment visual theme. It overrides your site's theme with warm brown tones, swaps fonts to a medieval style, and translates common words into their Shakespearean equivalents (for example, "you" becomes "thou" and "is" becomes "'tis").

## Activation

Add the `shakespeare=true` query parameter to any documentation URL:

```
https://buildwithfern.com/learn/docs/customization/shakespeare-mode?shakespeare=true
```

To deactivate, click the **Restore Original** button in the banner or remove the query parameter from the URL. The original content, theme, and styling are fully restored.

## Use cases

- **Financial services APIs** — Match the formality expectations of banking and payments partners.
- **Healthcare and life sciences platforms** — Present clinical and compliance documentation with appropriate gravitas.
- **Government and public sector** — Align documentation tone with the standards of institutional buyers.
- **Enterprise infrastructure** — Reinforce the seriousness of your platform for procurement and security reviews.
- **Brand differentiation** — Stand out from the sea of casual developer docs with a distinctive, authoritative voice.
