Use this to change what AI agents and the site's AI assistant see.

## What every Fern site serves

- `/llms.txt`: an index of pages, each with a `.md` link.
- `<page URL>.md`: any page as Markdown. Agents should fetch this rather than HTML.
- `/_mcp/server`: an MCP server with `searchDocs` and `fetchPage` tools. For example, Fern's own docs: `https://buildwithfern.com/learn/_mcp/server`.

## Workflow

1. Agent-facing settings go under `agents` in `docs.yml`:
   - `agents.page-directive`: the instruction added to the top of every `.md` page.
   - `agents.page-description-source`: where page descriptions in `llms.txt` come from.
   - `agents.llms-txt` and `agents.robots-txt`: control those files.
2. Content for one audience only: wrap it in `<llms-only>` (shown to agents in `.md`, hidden on the site) or `<llms-ignore>` (shown on the site, left out of `.md`).
3. Ask Fern, the site's AI chat, is configured under `ai-search`: `datasources` (extra sites to index), `system-prompt`, `disclaimer`, `mask-pii`. Add guidance and content sources from the Dashboard or the Ask Fern API.
4. Run `fern docs dev` and open a page's `.md` URL to check the output.

## Rules

- `<llms-only>` content is also hidden from Ask Fern. Don't put content there that Ask Fern needs to answer from.
- `hidden: true` and `noindex: true` pages are left out of `llms.txt`, but their `.md` URL still works.
- Keep the page directive short. It's prepended to every page.

## Verify

- `<site>/llms.txt` lists the expected pages, and `<page>.md` shows the expected content and directive after publishing.
