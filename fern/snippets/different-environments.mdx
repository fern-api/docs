Configure different {{type}} for production versus internal APIs:

```yaml title="generators.yml"
groups:
  production:
    specs:
      - openapi: openapi.yml
        {{type}}: production-{{type}}.yml
    generators:
      ...
  internal:
    specs:
      - openapi: openapi.yml
        {{type}}: internal-{{type}}.yml
    generators:
      ...
```
